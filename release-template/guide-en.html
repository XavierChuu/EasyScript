EasyScript for Premiere Pro

Auto silence-cut · transcription · speaker labeling · subtitles — directly on your timeline. Installation & User Guide

1. Requirements

  • Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 or newer (macOS or Windows).
  • About 3 GB free disk space (the transcription model downloads on first use — needs internet).
  • Apple Silicon Macs (M1–M5) are fastest; Intel/Windows machines also work.

2. Installation

Your package contains: EasyScript-Premiere.zxp, a backend/ folder, the installers, and this guide.

macOS

  1. Fully quit Premiere (Cmd + Q).
  2. Right-click install-mac.command → Open → Open (macOS asks once on first run).
  3. The script installs the panel and the backend automatically. Reopen Premiere when it finishes.

Windows

  1. Fully quit Premiere.
  2. Right-click install-win.bat → Run as administrator.
  3. Reopen Premiere when it finishes.
Open the panel: Window → Extensions → EasyScript. On first open the panel starts the backend automatically (top corner shows Connecting → Connected). The first transcription downloads the model (needs internet, a few minutes).

3. Workflow

StepAction
1 Pick a sourceSelect a clip on the timeline (or set In/Out marks). Range: Selected Clip / In / Out Points / Entire Timeline.
2 Load AudioClick Load Audio from Timeline → the waveform appears (exactly the selected part, including trimmed clips and nested sequences).
3 Detect SilenceFinds silent gaps. Click the ⚙ next to "Waveform" to tune sensitivity (padding, dB threshold, min silence).
4 TranscribeSpeech to text. Pick the Model & Language in Settings (⚙).
5 SpeakersIdentify who is speaking (needs an HF Token in Settings — see section 4).
6 Apply CutRemoves silence directly on the timeline (ripples clips closed and keeps audio/video in sync).
7 Make SubtitleCreates subtitles on the timeline. A dialog lets you pick the original + any translations. Line breaks follow the "Display mode" in Segments.
8 Label SpeakerCuts at speaker changes & renames each clip to its speaker name.

4. Settings (⚙ button)

  • Model / Language: choose the transcription model and language.
  • Speakers — HF Token: a free HuggingFace token is required for speaker identification. Get one at huggingface.co/settings/tokens and accept the terms of both models: pyannote/speaker-diarization-3.1 and pyannote/segmentation-3.0.
  • Translation: choose a translation provider (NLLB-200 / Ollama / Hy-MT2 / Claude API). Download the local model in Settings if using a local provider.
  • Subtitle timing: subtitles timed after the cut or to the original.

5. Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
"EasyScript" not in ExtensionsRun the installer again, then fully quit & reopen Premiere.
Panel stays OfflineThe backend isn't up. Reopen the panel (it auto-starts the backend). The first run may take a few minutes to download the model.
Speakers shows a token errorEnter your HF Token & accept the two pyannote model terms (section 4), then Save Settings.
Nested audio sounds emptyMake sure the sequence has audio; try again — the render is auto-normalized.
Note: EasyScript never modifies your source files. All cuts/renames apply to clips on the timeline — you can Undo (Cmd/Ctrl + Z) at any time.
